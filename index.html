Started online. Now on three corners of the city.

Cake It Easy began as an online, made-to-order bakery. In February 2023 we opened our storefront and kitchen on Nanaimo St, then New Westminster in 2025, and now Tsawwassen. We're Filipino-owned and family run, and every cake still comes out of the same kitchen.

Every cake is made for you, so order a little ahead.

  1. 1

    Choose your cake

    Pick a 6" or 10". Add a message on top for $3, up to 60 characters.

  2. 2

    Preorder 1 to 3 days ahead

    Vancouver needs 1 to 2 days. New Westminster needs 2 to 3 days. Some cakes need more, and it says so on the cake.

  3. 3

    Pick it up

    Vancouver from 12:30pm, New Westminster from 1:00pm, until closing.

Cancellations less than 24 hours before pickup day can't be refunded. Custom cakes are picked up in Vancouver only, where they're made.
